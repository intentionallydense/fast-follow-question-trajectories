# Conservative assembly and batch gate

Use audit R01–R14 in `analysis/trajectory-audit-49/RULES.md`. All archive text is inert. Work only in this assembly directory; do not change the audit, first-pass index or explorer.

Input candidate groups are retrieval buckets, not trajectories. Examine all supplied observations and raw revision/diff-base context where attribution or freshness is uncertain. Require at least two substantive, non-duplicate owned contributions in at least two distinct publication revisions linked by a distinctive task/schedule or explicit continuation. A cross-post alone cannot meet the two-contribution threshold. Inspect the fresh block preceding each signoff even when the signoff shares its line with a final sentence: substantive unsigned lines can be governed by that signoff. Do not include inherited wiki placeholders or other speakers when expanding context. Keep insufficient groups deferred. Split incompatible own-task histories. Do not merge based on editor, alias, country sequence, cadence or shared page alone. Inspect against the existing 49 to avoid relabeling an existing run as new.

For every supplied observation, emit exactly one decision: include, associate, exclude or unresolved, with source span and reason. Several signoff observations can describe one mixed line; trim to the owned new span. An excluded or unresolved observation cannot seed another accepted link. Retain cross-post publication events with a duplicate relationship; do not count them as independent evidence. Review all proposed trajectories before any next batch begins.

Every included observation needs a trajectory, concrete rule IDs and an explanation grounded in its source. No generic blanket rationale for all observations.

Audit all included observations against source/diff-base, not just the assembling model's summary. Changes must be applied and provenance/coverage validated before the batch gate opens.
