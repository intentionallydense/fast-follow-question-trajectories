# Batch004: 45 new trajectories and one existing-history extension

Fifty queued candidate groups were assembled, then independently reviewed by a different reviewer for each partition. The accepted result is **45 new reported histories**, **one extension to the existing Aug02 history**, and **four deferred groups**. All 287 supplied observations have an assembly decision and an independent source review.

The cumulative assembly now contains 192 new reported trajectories from 200 candidate groups; with the original 49, there are 241 reported histories (including one provisional baseline history). These counts do not authenticate distinct agents.

## Inclusion and exclusion standard

The initial audit’s [R01–R14 rules](../../research/RULES-v1.md) are byte-identical to the original. The [assembly protocol](../../PROTOCOL.md) additionally requires two substantive, nonduplicate owned contributions in two distinct publication revisions. Names, editor labels, shared pages, common sequences and copied claims cannot establish membership alone. Reviewers inspected original archive bodies, diff bases and relevant preceding signed blocks; forecasts, peer reports, copied publications and unresolved conflicts remain distinct. The later 147-trajectory audit’s attribution and clock-typing lessons were also applied.

## Independent audit and corrections

All 50 groups were proposed before review began. Assemblers A/B/C were reviewed by C/A/B respectively. Frozen `proposed-1.json` through `proposed-3.json` preserve the original proposals; the three audit files and [consolidated resolution](audit-resolution.json) preserve the resulting changes.

The audit recorded eleven corrections across ten candidate groups:

- Hold one RNG research post unresolved because it lacks a specific bridge to the writer’s timed history.
- Trim two owned spans to remove a peer’s R3 sentence and an inherited closing request.
- Add three relationships marking later publications as restatements of already recorded events.
- Preserve the Jul30 forecast’s explicit `21:48:33, likely :34` alternative.
- Link the Oct18 observed one-second timing correction to its earlier forecast.
- Restore three explicit R1 task-date annotations from the source.

No supported trajectory core was removed in the audit. Audit verdicts were 39 pass, 10 revise and one defer; three other groups were already deferred by their assemblers and passed review in that disposition.
